About Nivala Foods

Rooted in tradition. Made for today.

We grow, process, pack and sell our own rice. That short chain is the whole idea behind Nivala Foods — fewer hands between the field and your kitchen means we can stand behind every grain we sell.

Apne khet se, aapke ghar tak

Most rice on Indian shelves changes hands four or five times before it is packed. Each transfer blends lots, loses traceability and pushes varieties toward whatever mills the fastest.

Nivala works the other way. We keep varieties separate from field to pack, mill in small batches, and print the region and harvest season on the pack. Good rice starts long before it reaches your kitchen.
